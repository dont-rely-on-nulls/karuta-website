#+begin_section .pillars
#+begin_pillar
{{{eyebrow(Store)}}}
{{{h3(Relations\, not tables)}}}

Declare a relation once with ~:- table~ and Sakura persists every fact you assert into it.
#+end_pillar

#+begin_pillar
{{{eyebrow(Ask)}}}
{{{h3(One language)}}}

Query stored facts with Karuta rules. Recursive queries and joins are ordinary rules.
#+end_pillar

#+begin_pillar
{{{eyebrow(Spread)}}}
{{{h3(Across nodes)}}}

Sakura runs inside your BEAM cluster, so facts live next to the processes that reason about them.
#+end_pillar
#+end_section

{{{h2(Why the name)}}}

In a round of karuta, a reader recites a poem from one card. The players race to find the card on the table that completes it and take it.

A Karuta program plays the same game. Your question is read aloud, and the engine searches the table of facts and rules for every card that matches.

#+attr_html: :class map
- Reading card :: The query you ask
- Grabbing cards :: Facts and rules in your program
- Taking a card :: Unification: a match binds the variables
- Many players :: Search branches running as BEAM processes
#+end_div

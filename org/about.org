{{{h3(Logical and Denotational)}}}

Pay attention to *what* actually matters to solve your problem.
Let the runtime figure out the *how*; operational details are completely
unrelated and is a concern of the language implementers.
#+end_div

#+begin_div
#+attr_html: :class deco :loading lazy

{{{h3(Uniformity)}}}

Application-layer and persistance-layer predicates are /semantically/ the same.
Therefore, we have a *uniform* syntax to deal with both. No string manipulation and no ORMs; built-in from the start.
#+end_div

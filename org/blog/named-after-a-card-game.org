In karuta, a reader recites the first half of a poem and players race to take the card that completes it. Logic programming works the same way: you ask a question and the engine looks for every fact and rule that completes it.

We kept that picture in mind while designing the language. The query is the reading card. Clauses are grabbing cards. Unification is the moment a hand lands on the right card.

* Four cards

The story on the home page follows four cards: a blossom after winter, a rebuilt nest, an owl that remembers, and a flight into open sky. Each one marks a step in how Karuta came to be.

Well-documented source code /assumes/ the existence of prior decisions. You will know its intricate
details, what its arguments mean, and what it does, but you will hit a block: why does this function exist?
Why is it doing things like /this/ instead of like /that/? How does it fit in the bigger picture of
this module? To answer /those/ is the purpose of this section:
pearls of wisdom, experience reports, and tutorials from what we are building, explaining the
top-level foundations that trigger all the code that is written.

Knowing Functional Programming and OCaml's specific abstractions combined with these posts
should be enough anyone to start! Let these posts vanish your fear of contributing! If you need more, we are more
than willing to help you via e-mail or in our Discord server!

#+DATE: 2026-09-10
#+FILETAGS: :Tutorial:
#+DESCRIPTION: How a variable gets its value, one card at a time.
#+KANA: むすび
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil title:nil

When you ask ~origin(sakura, What)~, Karuta looks for clauses whose head can be made equal to your question. ~What~ is an empty slot. Any card that fits fills it.

#+begin_src karuta
grows(sakura, nest).
?- grows(sakura, What).
   What = nest.
#+end_src

If a card does not fit, the engine puts it back and tries the next one. That is backtracking.
